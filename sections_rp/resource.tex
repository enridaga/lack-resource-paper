\section{Details, Availability, and Maintenance}
\label{sec:resource}

\begin{sloppypar}The LACK ontology and knowledge graph is published as Linked Open Data under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence. Version 1.1 was released in April 2026 and is accessible at \url{https://climatesense-project.eu/lack/} as a Turtle dump (\texttt{KG.ttl}), a live SPARQL endpoint powered by QLever\footnote{\url{https://sparql.climatesense.kmi.tools/climatesense}}, and an interactive exploration interface.
Table~\ref{tab:kg-stats} summarises the key statistics; a full breakdown by property is on the LACK website.
The ontology is separately available in Turtle and OWL Manchester Syntax under the persistent namespace \url{https://purl.net/climatesense/lack/ns#}.
The dataset is archived on Zenodo at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19846035} with a VoID/DCAT description (\texttt{lack-dataset.ttl}).
The resource is maintained by KMi, rebuilt annually, and guaranteed available for at least five years under funder open data policy.
\paragraph{Disclaimer.} LACK reorganises information from third-party investigative sources; the authors do not endorse or assume responsibility for the original claims, and the relations published surface structural connections rather than allegations against any named party.
\end{sloppypar}
